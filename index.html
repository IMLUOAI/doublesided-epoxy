FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what most customers ask before booking. Don't see your question? Give us a call and we'll walk you through it.

Most residential garages are completed in 1–2 days, including prep, coating, and cure time before light foot traffic. Larger commercial spaces typically take 2–4 days. We'll walk you through the exact schedule in your written estimate so there are no surprises.
A properly installed epoxy floor lasts 15–20 years or more in residential settings, and 5–10 years in heavy industrial use. Our premium systems include a UV-stable topcoat that resists yellowing, chemical exposure, and abrasion. We back every installation with a written workmanship warranty.
Yes. We partner with a major financing provider to offer 0% APR plans for qualified buyers, typically over 12 months. Longer-term plans are also available. We can pre-qualify you in minutes during your quote — no impact to your credit score.
Once fully cured, epoxy is non-toxic, hypoallergenic, and easy to keep clean — it does not harbor dust mites, mold, or pet dander like carpet does. We use low-VOC materials during installation and recommend keeping pets and kids out of the work area for 24–72 hours while curing.
We handle all the prep — that includes clearing the space, diamond-grinding or shot-blasting the concrete for proper adhesion, repairing cracks and pitting, and degreasing the surface. The only thing we ask is that you remove valuables and personal items from the area before our crew arrives.
Our standard installations come with a 3-year workmanship warranty; premium flake and metallic finishes carry a 5-year warranty. This covers delamination, peeling, and adhesion failure under normal use. The warranty does not cover damage from cuts, gouges, or abuse, but most issues are easily repaired at low cost.
